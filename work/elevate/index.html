    Elevate

    A home in Dubai or Cairo, a payment plan you can live with, and a company of your own, in one calm mobile app.

    Role
    UI/UX designer
    Type
    Freelance project
    Platform
    iOS & Android
    Markets
    UAE · Egypt
    Scope
    50 screens
    Going up↑
    01The brief

    Elevate sells and rents homes in the UAE and Egypt. It also helps people moving to the UAE set up a company and get residency. One app had to carry both businesses, without either one burying the other.

    A

    People looking for a home

    They compare compounds, developers and, above all, payment plans: the down payment, the monthly amount, how many years, and when the keys arrive.

    B

    People starting a company

    New to the UAE, they don't know what a business setup involves. They want to know what they need and roughly what it will cost before they talk to anyone.

    02Blueprint

    Four rooms, one front door.

    The app is planned like a building. Everyone comes in through the same lobby: pick a country, then a service. From there, four areas hold all fifty screens, and the side menu is the corridor between them.

    03Arrival

    Two taps, then only what matters.

    Egypt or the UAE, then real estate or business services. The next button waits until a choice is made. People who pick business services go straight from "what do I need?" to a price, in three short steps.

    04The home screen

    Search first, then reasons to browse.

    1. Search on topCity, purpose, price range and type, before anything else.
    2. Proof, earlyA campaign banner, then developers, projects and properties in numbers.
    3. Recommended compoundsPrice "from", the unit mix, and a quick call or WhatsApp.
    4. Top compoundsBig photo cards with the number of properties inside.
    5. Recommended propertiesBeds, baths and size on every card.
    6. Top areasBrowse by area, with compounds and properties counted.
    7. Buy or rentOne switch: all, sell, rent.
    8. Properties by cityA wall of city chips over the skyline.
    9. New projectsLaunches straight from the developers.
    10. List yours, or talk to usThe two other jobs of the app close the page.
    05Search & filters

    The way buyers actually decide.

    Results switch between a list and a map, and every card carries a Compare button. An empty search says so plainly. "More filter" goes past bedrooms and price: here, people choose by payment plan and delivery date as much as by size. Move over a screen to look closer.

    06The unit

    Every number before the call.

    One long page answers what people would otherwise phone about. Here it is, unrolled.

    01 The gallery with a +24 count, the price, and Call or WhatsApp straight away.

    02 About this home, the master plan to download, a video, and the full property details.

    03 The map, then payment plans: cash next to instalments, with the down payment and each year.

    04 Apply now in a short form, then similar listings to compare against.

    Compare, side by side

    Add units from the results, and a compare bar rises from the bottom. The comparison then lines them up row by row:

    • Developer
    • Location
    • Price
    • Down payment
    • Instalments
    • Years of instalments
    • Floor area
    • Bedrooms
    • Bathrooms
    • Finishing
    • Type
    • Delivery date
    07The company

    Trust first, then a request.

    About us, careers with a CV upload, branches in both countries, a partner form, and a project quote in three steps with a review screen that says when the answer will come. One header, one breadcrumb, one footer, so it reads as one place.

    08The corridor

    Everything else, one menu away.

    The side menu is the corridor between the four rooms: home, search, career, about and contact, the country switch with its flag, and the account below. Signed out, it leads with "Be our partner" and Login; signed in, with the profile.

    • Accounts Sign up and sign in with email or social accounts, a four-digit code, and a full reset-password flow.
    • Profile Favourites, edit profile, change password, notifications.
    • Leaving Delete account asks twice and says clearly that it can't be undone. Even the 404 page leads home.
    09Materials

    Deep teal, warm sand.

    The splash

    Seven frames: a white circle rises through the teal, fills the screen, falls away to the corner, and leaves the mark. The intro of this page is built from it.
