01

Context & problem — Investing in real estate felt out of reach

For most people in Saudi Arabia, owning part of a commercial building meant large tickets, paperwork and little visibility once the money was in. Funds and sukuk existed, but finding one, understanding its terms and following its returns happened across calls, PDFs and bank transfers.

Sahem set out to bring the whole cycle into one product: sign up, verify, compare opportunities, invest from a few hundred riyals and watch the investment grow.

06

Designing the journey — From first tap to first return

Five steps, each built so the investor always knows where they are and what happens next.

01

Sign in & sign up

The first screen sets the tone

On the web, sign-in sits beside a full-height brand panel: the wallet in an iPhone, a QR code to get the app, and the Capital Market Authority licence. In the app, sign-in is a phone number with Face ID for returning users, and sign-up asks one question first, individual or company, then shows the password rules as you type.

02

Verify

OTP · KYC

A one-time code confirms the phone number. KYC is broken into short, single-topic questions, and people who want to look around first can skip it and continue as a guest.

03

Discover

Funds and sukuk, side by side

Opportunities and sukuk sit in two tabs on home. Every card leads with the amount raised, the progress bar and the fund's status. The details page answers the rest, with units, dates, duration and a return calculator, before anyone commits.

04

Invest

Every riyal visible before you pay

A unit slider sets the amount, and the receipt itemises the platform fee, discounts, VAT and the total before payment. Paying with Apple Pay or mada takes one tap, and a clear success screen leads straight to the invoice.

05

Track

Portfolio, wallet and payouts in one place

The portfolio sums up total investment, returns and the number of opportunities, with a certificate for each holding. The wallet keeps the balance and bank details together, so a withdrawal is a single form.

07

How to invest — The landing page's guided tour

Four steps from the landing page, each a layered scene: a live screen in an iPhone with the key moment pulled out on a floating card. Scroll through them below.

  1. 01Get Started

    Use Sahem’s mobile app or website to register as an investor and manage your account.

  2. 02Fund Your Wallet

    Recharge your Sahem e-wallet, explore available investment opportunities, and get notified when new ones open.

  3. 03Invest with Confidence

    Invest in opportunities that match your goals after reviewing their terms and conditions.

  4. 04Track & Manage

    Track your portfolio, receive updates from fund managers, and recharge or withdraw funds anytime.

08

Web platform — The same journey at desktop size

On desktop, navigation moves into a side menu and the opportunity cards sit in a grid. The details page uses the extra width to show the fund's facts and the investment panel side by side.

09

Admin dashboard — Running the platform from one screen

The Sahem team manages investors, funds, KYC questions, distributions, notifications and roles. The home view leads with the totals they check daily, with investor demographics underneath.

10

Outcome — A complete platform, ready to ship

The design was handed over as one system covering the investor app, the web platform, the landing page and the admin panel, built on one set of colours, type and components.
