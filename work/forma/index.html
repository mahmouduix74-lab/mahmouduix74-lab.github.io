01

Context & problem — The talent is there, the path is missing

Young players train hard, but their next step still depends on chance and a small personal network. Coaches, clubs and events sit in different places, and a player’s record — match stats, physical assessments, certificates — rarely travels with them. Without one trusted digital reference, talent gets lost and club scouts have little reliable data to go on.

Forma brings that path into one web platform: players build a complete sports profile ready for scouts, book verified trainers and events, and grow with a plan that fits their level.

02

The solution — One web platform, two connected sides

Forma is designed as a desktop web platform, built for dense data, analytics and booking management. Players and trainers each get their own portal, built from one system, and a player’s profile can be shared as a link with nothing to install.

03

Sign in & sign up — As little as possible at every step

The whole entry flow sits on night-stadium art, so the platform’s sporting identity is there from the first second. To cut drop-off and effort, each step asks for as little as possible. Scroll through the four steps below.

  1. 01Sign in

    One field, the email. Players who already have an account go straight in.

  2. 02Create account

    New players start from the same single field.

  3. 03Verify email

    A four-digit code, a 59:00 timer and a one-tap resend.

  4. 04Player profile

    Name, nationality, main position on the pitch, and an optional invite code that links the player to a trainer.

04

Home — Chase the Glory

One page that answers the player’s question, “what’s my next step?”: a hero carousel (get discovered, train with verified coaches, unlock more with Forma plans), one search across trainers, events and clubs, upcoming events, the plans on a dark stadium band, elite verified trainers and the players’ community feed. Scroll to move through it.

05

Book a trainer — From profile to paid in five steps

The player browses verified trainers, checks their licences, past clubs and session plans, then sends a booking request. Payment comes only after the trainer accepts, so a player never pays for a session that isn’t confirmed.

Events — Spots, price and payment on one screen

Trials and training camps show the date, pitch, price and spots left up front. One payment pattern runs across the whole platform — a session with a trainer, a seat at an event or a plan upgrade — with cards, Apple Pay, PayPal or the Forma wallet, and VAT shown before paying.

07

Plans — Three levels, compared at a glance

Basic, Platinum and Golden share the same card layout, so the benefits compare at a glance. Golden is lifted and turned blue to draw the eye. Every plan lists the same four benefits: priority trials, an AI performance index, pro verification and direct scout chat.

08

Progress — Wallet, leaderboard and certificates

A profile is worth as much as the trust in its numbers. Around the player’s stats sit a digital wallet with easy top-ups and a detailed transaction history, a competitive leaderboard ranked by performance, and the certificates the player adds and manages.
