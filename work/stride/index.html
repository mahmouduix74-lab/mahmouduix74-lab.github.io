01

The challenge — One club, three kinds of people

Members need to buy a package and then look after it themselves: freeze it, transfer it, upgrade it, and book classes, personal trainers, single sessions and InBody tests.

Trainers need to see their day, check in, mark attendance and upload InBody results. The club's staff run classes, timetables, trainers, packages, coupons and payments, including split and partial payments, across branches.

02

Research — Listening first, then mapping every product

Before any screen, we spoke to the people who would use the platform through interviews and a survey, then mapped what each product had to hold and how people would move through it.

03

The approach — One path for every booking

A class, a trainer, a single session and an InBody test all follow the same four steps: choose, pick a date and time, pay, and get a clear confirmation with every detail of the order.

Once a member has booked one thing, they know how to book everything else.

04

Design system — Warm gold on quiet surfaces

Gold carries every action, a soft beige holds secondary surfaces, and green is kept for good news: an active plan, a successful payment, a trainer who has checked in. Everything else stays neutral, so the photos of the club do the talking.

05

First steps — From splash to signed in

A dark splash and three onboarding screens set the mood with real club photos. Then a short sign-in with email and password, sign up, a one-time code, and a full forgot-password flow. Guests can look around before they create an account.

06

Member home — The day at a glance

Home opens with the member's branch, the club's current offers, the subscription status with one tap to manage it, and the next events. Classes show free and paid counts with quick filters by type, and personal trainers list their specialty and price per session next to a Book now button.

07

Subscriptions — Freeze, transfer, upgrade without the front desk

Each change is a short, self-serve flow. Freezing explains what pauses and how the end date moves, with a 14-day limit shown up front. Transferring validates the recipient by phone or email before anything happens. Upgrading shows the new end date, the price and what the member gains.

08

InBody & bookings — Everything booked, in one list

InBody sessions show what is left at a glance: total, free, purchased, remaining and completed. My bookings gathers classes, InBody, single sessions and personal training in one place, filtered by status, and any booking can be rescheduled from the same date and time sheet.

09

Trainer app — The day starts with check-in

The trainer's home stays locked until they check in at the branch. After that it fills with the day: counts for personal training, InBody and classes, and each session with a Start session button.

The schedule shows bookings by month and the trainer's own availability, and attendance is marked client by client. Supervisors get extra tools to manage sessions and assign trainers.

10

Admin dashboard — Running the club

Staff sign in to a dashboard that manages the same things members see: classes, clubs, trainers, packages, single sessions, coupons, InBody and client subscriptions. Every list has search and filters, capacity and status at a glance, and a timetable by day, week or month that exports to Excel.

11

Outcome — Two apps and a dashboard in two months

The platform was delivered as one system: a member app, a trainer app and an admin dashboard on one set of colours, type and components.
