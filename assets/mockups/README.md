# Mockups with the real screens

Eleven scenes picked by the designer (numbers P1–P56 from his PSD sheet) from his own Dropbox folder of free mockups.

- Rendered from the PSDs themselves: each smart object's contents replaced with a real screen, so the file's own
  warp, glare, shadows and blend modes are kept. Tool: `tools/pea.js` (Photopea). P32 and P34 are too large for
  Photopea in this environment and are rebuilt from their own layers with the same blend modes (`tools/scenes/psd_layered.py`).
- P8, P12, P14 and P21 were also picked, but their zips in the Dropbox folder are incomplete (the PSD inside is cut off),
  so they cannot be opened. They need a fresh copy of the original files.
- Source mockup files are not in the repo.
